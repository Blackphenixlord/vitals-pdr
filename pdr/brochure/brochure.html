The problem

Earth is 40 minutes away

Earth-based symptom checkers assume Earth. In deep space, a stuffy nose on day 2 is fluid shift, not a cold — and joint pain after a spacewalk is decompression sickness until proven otherwise.

When the doctor is 20 minutes away, the crew still needs an answer now.

With communication blackouts of up to two weeks, a crew with no physician aboard has to decide what to do. VITALS answers three questions:

What could this be?

Conditions from a research-grounded knowledge base — no internet.

How serious is it?

Urgency first, so the crew knows if a ground call is needed.

What do we do now?

Immediate steps and a safety backstop for non-medical crewmembers.
