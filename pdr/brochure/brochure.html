The problem
When the doctor is 20 minutes away, the crew still needs an answer now.

Symptom checkers on Earth assume Earth. In space a stuffy nose on day 2 is fluid shift, not a cold, and joint pain after a spacewalk is decompression sickness until proven otherwise.

With blackouts of up to two weeks and no physician on board, a non-medical crewmember has to answer three questions alone.

VITALS

An onboard medical assistant for astronauts on long missions to the Moon and Mars. It works with no internet at all.

Team TETHERNASA HUNCH 2026–27
What it does

Built to be trusted with no doctor in the room

Talk in plain words

Type how you feel, the way you’d say it out loud.

Urgency comes first

Every answer leads with how serious it is, in a word and a color.

Every answer has a source

It only names conditions from 35 researched spaceflight conditions, and shows where each came from.

A second check that never sleeps

A rules engine can raise the alarm even when the AI doesn’t.

The crew can correct it
