01 · PROBLEM STATEMENT

Earth is 40 minutes away

On the ISS a flight surgeon is one radio call away. On Mars the delay is 4 to 40 minutes each way, with blackouts NASA plans for of up to two weeks — and usually no physician on board.

Crews need an onboard system a non-medical crewmember can talk to in plain words — offline, space-aware, clear about when to call for help, and showing where every answer came from.

02 · RESEARCH 1

There's no data to train on

NASA's own risk models say in-flight medical data is severely limited — for some serious conditions the in-flight count is zero. So VITALS grounds a pretrained model in cited research instead of training one.
